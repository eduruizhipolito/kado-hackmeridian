# Demo video script (≤ 2 min)

Goal: in under two minutes, a judge sees the problem, a real opening on a phone,
and why only Stellar makes it this simple. Same one-liner as the README and the
landing page (DESIGN.md).

## Before recording

- **Codes:** use the 3 Kadós reserved for the video, in
  `batches/video-202610260202.codes.csv` (on your machine, git-ignored). Valid
  until 2 Nov. Don't use the judges' codes from the README. One take uses one code;
  you have two spares for retakes.
- **Phone:** mobile data or good wifi, brightness up, notifications off, browser
  tab already on https://kado-hackmeridian.vercel.app. Zoom at 100%.
- **Second tab (laptop):** stellar.expert ready to show the receipt bigger, and
  the README "How Stellar is used" section.
- **Recording:** screen-record the phone (iOS/Android built-in recorder) and
  voice over it, or film the phone in hand. Landscape laptop shots only for the
  explorer and the diagram.

## Script

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Landing hero on the phone: logo + one-liner | "Kadó: gift digital dollars to anyone, no wallet needed." |
| 0:08–0:25 | Stay on the hero (or a photo of a meetup in Peru) | "At outreach events in Peru, people are curious about Stellar, but they leave the room the same way they came in: no wallet, no account, no funds. Asking them to install an app and guard a seed phrase just to receive a gift doesn't work." |
| 0:25–0:35 | Type the code in the box (or open the link), land on the closed Kadó | "With Kadó, the event gives each attendee a code. They open it on their phone. No app, no sign-up." |
| 0:35–0:45 | "The event left you a Kadó." Tap **Open**, coral bar runs along the seam | "The amount stays hidden. They tap Open…" |
| 0:45–0:55 | Lid lifts: **$2.00 — It's yours.** | "…and in one transaction, about five seconds, they have their own Stellar account with real funds. They paid nothing, not even the fee." |
| 0:55–1:10 | Continue → balance → **View receipt** → stellar.expert account page (show on laptop if small) | "The receipt is the account itself, on the public explorer: the balance, and the organizer sponsoring its reserve. Anyone can verify it." |
| 1:10–1:35 | README diagram: the 4 operations, or the landing "Built on Stellar's native features" strip | "This uses two native Stellar features, no smart contract. Claimable balances hold the gift for an account that doesn't exist yet, and return it to the organizer if nobody opens it. Sponsored reserves let the new account start at zero. Both run in one atomic transaction: either the account is created and the Kadó opens, or nothing happens." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Back to the phone balance screen, or the README Market section | "For an organizer, a funded account costs about twenty cents of locked capital on top of the gift, and unopened Kadós come back. Our first users: the Stellar Ambassador Chapter in Peru." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Landing hero again, URL visible | "Kadó. Gift digital dollars to anyone, no wallet needed. Try it with the demo codes in our README." |

## Copy rules for the voice-over (THESIS.md)

Say "Kadó", "open", "digital dollars". Don't say voucher, coupon, redeem,
claim, crypto or token. ("Claimable balances" is fine: it's the protocol name.)
The giver is "the event", never a person.
