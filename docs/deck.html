How it works

Two native Stellar features. No smart contract.

Claimable balances · CAP-23The gift waits on-chain for an account that doesn't exist yet. Unopened? It returns to the organizer after expiry.
Sponsored reserves · CAP-33The organizer carries the new account's reserve, so the attendee starts at 0 XLM and the gift arrives whole.
■ Atomic: the account is created and the Kadó opens, or nothing happens. Retrying is always safe.
ONE TRANSACTION · 3 SIGNATURES

3 endSponsoringFutureReservesattendee
